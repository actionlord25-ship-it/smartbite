About SmartBite

SmartBite connects hungry people with local kitchens and the riders who bring the food. We built it so each side gets what it needs: customers get honest prices and live tracking, restaurants get tools to run their menu, staff and earnings, and riders get clear jobs and fair pay.

Every order is priced the same way: the food, a small service charge and the platform fee, all shown before you pay.
